# Fintech × Agent: From Information Access to Informed Investment Decisions

<small>FINANCIAL AI RESEARCH · October 27, 2026 · 4 domains / 15 topics</small>

AI makes information and analytical tools more accessible, but **access is not judgment, correct calculations are not research validity, and a proposed action is not authorization to execute it**. This collection focuses on securities investing and wealth management, not a general map of Agent Runtime features.

We select essays that **directly address a material financial-business problem**. Every essay keeps its one canonical classification in [modules](/en/modules), [architecture](/en/thinking) or [engineering](/en/engineering); this page never duplicates articles. Empty topics are deliberate.

## How Topics Grow—and Why Some Remain Empty

An empty topic means **no sufficiently deep, directly relevant published essay has qualified yet**, not that a product capability is necessarily absent. General engineering essays on Publication, Langfuse, Hooks, Tool Surface and recovery stay in their canonical collections instead of being forced into the finance selection.

**Each second-level topic features at most three essays.** At capacity, first review overlap and consider replacement or consolidation. Split only when genuinely distinct financial questions require separate explanations—not automatically because the limit is reached. Each essay has one primary topic placement here.

Industry essays can inspire research, but claims about win rates, error-free alerts, universal registration requirements or statutory record-retention periods need original-source, jurisdiction and sample checks. Implementation, limited tests, business results and legal compliance are different kinds of evidence.

The first research queue covers **financial research quality, finance-specific errors, brokerage-app AI and employee workflows**. Research completion is not a promise to publish a new essay or implement a product feature.
