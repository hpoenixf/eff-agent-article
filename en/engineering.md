# AI Agent Engineering Practice

How to build, validate, maintain and evolve complex systems with coding agents, without confusing passing tests, completed documents and delivered outcomes.

The [on-demand engineering methods](/en/principles) connect design review, staged delivery, evidence, diagnosis and retirement. The [staged weekly-review case study](/en/articles/agent-weekly-review-staged-delivery), [retiring obsolete engineering knowledge](/en/articles/027-agent-context-document-history), and [refactoring, retiring, or deleting tests when writing a Spec](/en/articles/test-lifecycle-refactoring-retirement-and-spec) provide concrete workflow lessons. See the [roadmap](/en/roadmap) for genuinely unfinished topics.

Start with a familiar requirement change: [When Tests Outgrow the Code: Helping Docs, Code, and Tests Evolve Together](/en/articles/docs-code-tests-evolve-together) uses an Excel-export teaching example to explain what changes, what remains protected, and how to inspect the final result.
