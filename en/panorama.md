# Panorama Series

Start with how the system works and how business rules change decisions, then explore complete engineering topics and the handoffs of information, collaboration and user experience. This selection includes both whole-system essays and topic panoramas such as observability, evaluation and Subagents. Each builds a coherent view through a complete reader problem and cross-stage relationships.

## Start with Your Current Problem

- New to agents? Start with [Agents in 15 Minutes](/en/articles/agent-in-15-minutes), then the [six-layer runtime panorama](/en/articles/017-prompt-to-answer-six-layer-panorama-v3).
- Distinguishing chat from execution? Use the [execution identity panorama](/en/articles/agent-task-session-run-worker-schedule) to follow runs, model turns, request attempts and tool actions, then identify what continues through follow-ups, retries and recovery.
- Designing a system? Start with [Nine Architecture Judgments Behind One Report](/en/articles/one-report-nine-architecture-judgments) to decide when to reuse, add or retire a mechanism.
- Exploring financial business and framework choices? Read the [financial agent panorama](/en/articles/financial-agent-system-panorama), then the financial-domain principles and four-way architecture comparison as needed.
- Designing context? Read [Principles](/en/articles/agent-context-governance-principles), then [Practice](/en/articles/agent-context-governance-actions).
- Designing a complete research experience? Choose [long-horizon research](/en/articles/agent-long-horizon-research-panorama), [end-to-end RAG](/en/articles/agent-knowledge-retrieval-end-to-end) or [client interaction](/en/articles/agent-client-interaction-panorama) to follow the task from input to delivery and subsequent use.

- Responsible for the quality of an AI-developed project? Start with the [AI Coding quality panorama](/en/articles/ai-coding-quality-panorama) to connect requirements, design, verification, review and delivery, then adopt the methods that fit your risks.

- Designing complete engineering methods? Follow the [observability panorama](/en/articles/025-agent-langfuse-from-trace-to-trustworthy-observability) through collection, diagnosis and readback; the [evaluation panorama](/en/articles/honest-agent-evaluation) through samples, scoring and actual delivery; and the [Subagent panorama](/en/articles/agent-subagent-context-specialization) through delegation, independent context and result handoff.
