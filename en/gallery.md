# Architecture Gallery

The [architecture gallery](/gallery) maps system capability owners, dependencies and article entry points. The interactive diagram currently uses Chinese labels; this English page is a guided entry, not a claim that the source diagram has been translated.

The gallery now identifies **41 capability owners**, including A35 Content Security and Trust. Its dedicated [English case study](/en/articles/agent-content-trust-prompt-injection) explains why source data must not silently become system instructions. The visual labels remain Chinese and are not presented as a fully translated gallery.

The gallery's capability owners are not additional architectural principles. Use the [core principles and engineering methods](/en/principles) for general decisions and the [writing roadmap](/en/roadmap) for forthcoming coverage.

For textual introductions, start with [architecture thinking](/en/thinking) or the [complete catalog](/en/catalog).
