### Updated scope — October 24, 2026

With the October 24 inventory decision, the interactive gallery now has **43 distinct capability Owners**: A01–A37, B01–B05 and E04. A36 (temporal resolution after semantic selection) and A37 (bidirectional durable Human Interaction) are now first-class logical Owners rather than related notes on older nodes. A36 still lacks final P0 acceptance for a normal, qualified real-agent calendar flow; A37 has a bounded local provider-free close but production Human Park is disabled by default. Neither Owner overrides existing Authorization, Recovery, Effect, data-source or Publication effects. Durable Human Interaction Stages A–D have completed their stated local provider-free formal close and archive, including Child original-text coordination. Production Human Park remains disabled by default, and live model quality and deployment remain unverified. Read [the HIL mechanism](/en/articles/agent-durable-human-interaction-response-adoption), [A36 temporal semantics](/en/articles/agent-temporal-semantics-market-calendar) (implementation present; real-agent acceptance open), and [personal Shadow Credits](/en/articles/agent-personal-credits-shadow-accounting) (self-view tested; whole-spec close open).

The code paths are not the same as end-to-end acceptance: financial research completion, authenticated account and PostgreSQL recovery, real calendar-source usage, model quality and deployment retain separate evidence requirements. Open a related owner's detail in the Chinese interactive map for the narrower source-level status; this page remains a guide rather than a fully translated diagram.
